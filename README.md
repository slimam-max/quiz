

🎯 About the Project

Quizz⁺ is a solo project first published on November 12, 2026.

Many students struggle to find previous years' examination papers because some question papers are not available digitally. Even when they are digital, students often do not know where to find them. Examination papers are scattered across different platforms, making it difficult for students to collect and access them in one place for effective preparation.

I started Quizz⁺ to address this problem by bringing previous years' examination papers together on a single platform. My goal is to make exam preparation easier and help students practice what they have learned.

✨ Features
Access previous years' examination papers in one place.
Clear explanations for every question.
Opportunities for students to practice what they have studied.
Support for high school and university students.
🎓 Target Audience
High school students.
University students.
Students preparing for academic examinations.
